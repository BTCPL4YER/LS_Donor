LifeStream Donors

Why do we need your info?

We ask for your information to ensure a smooth and safe donation process. Your details help us:

  1. Verify Eligibility: We need to confirm your eligibility to donate blood based on health and donation history.
  2. Schedule Appointments: Your information allows us to schedule donation appointments that fit your availability and our operational needs.
  3. Contact and Communication: It enables us to communicate important details about your donation, such as health screenings, donation guidelines, and post-donation care.
  4. Ensure Donor Safety: Understanding your health history helps us ensure the safety of both donors and recipients of blood donations.
  5. Maintain Records: We maintain accurate records of donations and donor information to comply with regulatory requirements and for future donation purposes.

Your information is confidential and used solely for donation-related purposes, ensuring a positive experience for you and the recipients who benefit from your generous donation.
